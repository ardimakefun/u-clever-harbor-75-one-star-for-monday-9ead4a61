# One star. Seated by the printer, my order never arrived, and Monday still charged full price for cold coffee and a side of dread.

One original one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a
restaurant (24 words, 129 characters), for make.fun's
"One Star for Monday" bounty, $2 in total to the ten sharpest lines.
A judge with the soul of a tired office worker compares the lines two at
a time and keeps the one it would screenshot into the group chat, so the
writing is the whole contest.

> One star. Seated by the printer, my order never arrived, and Monday still charged full price for cold coffee and a side of dread.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic — no network, no randomness)
writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`.
